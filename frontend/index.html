CareSaathi — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Informational Policy Notice

At CareSaathi, family trust and healthcare privacy are our foundational principles. This Privacy Policy outlines our data governance and privacy practices.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, authenticated email address, family identifier, and role.
  • Family Health Records: Vitals logs, medication schedules, appointment records, and caregiver handoff notes.
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure OAuth tokens from Google identity verification.

2. How Family Information is Used

Information is used exclusively to facilitate care coordination within your designated family circle. We never sell, monetize, or broker healthcare data to third parties.

3. Family Access Boundaries & Role-Based Isolation

CareSaathi enforces strict multi-tenant cryptographic and database-level isolation. Secondary caregivers can only view records explicitly assigned to them by the Family Owner.

4. AI Assistant Context Filtering

The CareSaathi AI assistant only accesses health records within the authenticated user's permission scope. Medical queries are processed with strict privacy guardrails and general informational disclaimers.

5. Data Deletion & Account Rights

Family Owners may request complete account data deletion and profile removal at any time via Settings.

CareSaathi — Terms of Service

Effective Date: November 2026 | Terms of Platform Use

1. Purpose of CareSaathi

CareSaathi is a family health and elderly-care coordination platform designed to help family members and caregivers collaborate on schedules, vitals logs, and doctor appointments.

2. Non-Clinical & Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer

IMPORTANT MEDICAL DISCLAIMER: CareSaathi is NOT a medical diagnostic tool or emergency response service. The software, including the AI assistant and reminders, provides informational coordination only and does NOT constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult licensed medical professionals for clinical decisions.

3. User Responsibilities & Authorized Consent

Users must have legal authorization or family consent before entering family members' health data or assigning caregiver roles.

4. Medication Reporting

A "Reported Taken" status indicates user-recorded adherence and does not represent independent clinical verification of medication consumption.
